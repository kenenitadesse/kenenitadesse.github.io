# 🔬 Research Experience

This section details my research visits and training placements in pharmacovigilance, drug safety analytics, and biomedical natural language processing (NLP). These placements formed the applied core of my PhD research on advanced statistical methods for monitoring drug safety.

---

## 🇫🇷 Visiting Researcher — Pharmacovigilance & Biomedical NLP

**CRPV Bordeaux-DROM & Inserm U1219 Bordeaux Population Health**  
CHU de Bordeaux, France  
*January 1 – July 5, 2026*  
**Supervisor:** Prof. Francesco Salvo

### Context

The Bordeaux Pharmacovigilance Centre (CRPV Bordeaux-DROM) is one of the 31 French regional pharmacovigilance centres. During this 6-month visit, I worked within the Inserm U1219 Bordeaux Population Health team on the application of natural language processing (NLP) to pharmacovigilance using Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs) from the Bordeaux Regional Pharmacovigilance Centre.

### Research Activities

- **NLP for pharmacovigilance:** Applied NLP methods to ICSRs to support drug safety monitoring.
- **CamemBERT-Bio fine-tuning:** Fine-tuned CamemBERT-Bio transformer models for MedDRA coding verification.
- **Model comparison:** Compared fine-tuned CamemBERT-Bio with Support Vector Classifiers using fixed embeddings.
- **Class imbalance:** Investigated class weighting, focal loss, and oversampling techniques for rare event detection.
- **Rule-based NLP:** Developed a rule-based NLP system for automated Brighton classification of Guillain-Barré syndrome.
- **Clinical entity extraction:** Extracted cerebrospinal fluid protein, cell count, and nerve conduction study findings from clinical narratives.
- **Weekly meetings:** Held weekly progress meetings with the supervisor.
- **Manuscript collaboration:** Collaborated on two manuscripts arising from this work.

### Deliverables

- **Two manuscripts** (in preparation / under review):
  1. *Fine-Tuned CamemBERT-Bio Models to Verify MedDRA Coding Related to Guillain-Barré Syndrome in Individual Case Safety Reports Database.*
  2. *Automated Classification of Guillain-Barré Syndrome Using Brighton Criteria from Individual Case Safety Reports: A Rule-Based Approach for Pharmacovigilance.*
- **Accepted abstract** for oral communication at the IATDMCT/SFPT Congress, Rennes, France, September 27–30, 2026.

## 🇮🇹 Visiting Trainee — Pharmacovigilance & Drug Safety Analytics

**CRFV Veneto (Centro Regionale di Farmacovigilanza del Veneto)**  
University of Verona, Department of Diagnostics and Public Health, Italy  
*September 2024 – February 2025*  
**Supervisor:** Prof. Ugo Moretti

### Context

The CRFV Veneto is the regional pharmacovigilance centre for the Veneto region of Italy. This 6-month compulsory training period formed part of my PhD programme and gave me hands-on exposure to Italian and European pharmacovigilance systems, tools, and standards.

### Training and Research Activities

1. **Autocoding project:** Introduction to the autocoding project and Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reporting methods.
2. **Pharmacovigilance education:** Drug–drug interactions and ADR management using the Italian pharmacovigilance database.
3. **Disproportionality analysis:** Training on disproportionality analysis using spontaneous reporting data and contingency tables.
4. **MedDRA and ICH standards:** Introduction to MedDRA terminology and ICH E2B(R3) reporting standards.
5. **ADR report management:** ETL procedures, data processing, data quality assurance, and real-world evidence (RWE) analysis.
6. **ICSR standards:** Introduction to Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) standards and CRFVV tools.
7. **Prescriptive cascade:** Overview of prescriptive cascade methodologies and related software tools (PostgreSQL, Ruby, Redis, Directus).
8. **DSUR and VigiBase:** Overview of Development Safety Update Reports (DSUR) and VigiBase use in non-profit clinical trials.
9. **MagiCoder Pro:** Introduction to MagiCoder Pro (natural language processing for pharmacovigilance).
10. **Networked databases:** Post-marketing surveillance and vaccinovigilance (VigiVax).
11. **Comparative NLP analysis:** Conducted a comparative analysis of different NLP systems for extracting ADR information from medicinal product information.

### Skills Gained

- Italian and European pharmacovigilance systems
- Disproportionality analysis (PRR, ROR)
- MedDRA terminology and ICH E2B(R3)
- ICSR standards and CRFVV tools
- ETL procedures and data quality assurance
- VigiBase, VigiVax, MagiCoder Pro
- PostgreSQL, Ruby, Redis, Directus
- Comparative NLP system evaluation

## Course Descriptions

### Statistical Theory & Inference
- **Stat3121 - Statistical Theory of Distributions:** Probability distributions, moment generating functions, transformations, and sampling distributions.
- **Stat3122 - Statistical Inference:** Point estimation, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and likelihood theory.

### Multivariate & Advanced Methods
- **Stat3133 - Introduction to Multivariate Methods:** Principal component analysis, factor analysis, discriminant analysis, and clustering techniques.
- **Stat3131 - Econometrics:** Linear regression models, panel data, time series econometrics, and model diagnostics.

### Biostatistics & Demography
- **Stat3101 - Biostatistics and Epidemiology:** Study designs, measures of association, logistic regression, and survival analysis applications in health research.
- **Stat3142 - Demography:** Population dynamics, fertility, mortality, migration, and demographic methods.

### Statistical Computing
- **Stat2081 - Statistical Computing I:** Introduction to SPSS for data management and analysis.
- **Stat2082 - Statistical Computing II:** Data manipulation, statistical modeling, and visualization using R.

### Core Statistics
- **Stat1011 - Basic Statistics:** Descriptive statistics, probability, sampling distributions, and introductory inference.
- **Stat1012 - Introduction to Probability Theory:** Probability axioms, conditional probability, random variables, and expectation.
- **Stat1013 - Statistical Methods:** t-tests, ANOVA, correlation, regression, and nonparametric methods.
- **Stat2091 - Sampling Theory:** Simple random sampling, stratified sampling, cluster sampling, and sample size determination.

### Applied Statistics
- **Stat2102 - Time Series Analysis:** ARIMA models, forecasting, stationarity, and seasonal decomposition.
- **Stat3172 - Social and Economic Statistics:** Application of statistical methods to social and economic data.
